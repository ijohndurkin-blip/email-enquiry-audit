Enquiry Audit

Reads a business's enquiry mailboxes and shows how many enquiries came in, how quickly they were answered and how many were missed. It reads who each email is from and to, the subject and the time, never changes anything in the mailbox, and doesn't keep passwords. The only email text it reads is in website-form notifications (to find the customer's address) and, if you turn on AI, the first lines of possible enquiries and the business's first reply to each.

3 Working hours and time spent

Run an audit first. This estimates the minutes from the real replies: their length, and what work they needed (prices, quotes, dates, attachments).

Time is counted per reply because that's what a stopwatch measures: from opening the customer's email to pressing send. The audit counts how many replies the business wrote, so the hours a week, month and year come from real numbers. Email can't show the exact minutes, so time 3–5 replies with a stopwatch and adjust the figure if needed.

AI sorting is off. To turn it on, add an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY environment variable in Vercel and redeploy.
