Enquiry Audit

Reads a business's enquiry mailboxes and shows how many enquiries came in, how quickly they were answered and how many were missed. It reads who each email is from and to, the subject and the time, never changes anything in the mailbox, and doesn't keep passwords. The only email text it reads is in website-form notifications (to find the customer's address) and, if you turn on AI sorting, the first lines of possible enquiries.
